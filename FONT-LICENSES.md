# Fonts

Fonts bundled from Google Fonts for offline-friendly rendering:

- Amiri — https://fonts.google.com/specimen/Amiri/about
- Cormorant Garamond — https://fonts.google.com/specimen/Cormorant+Garamond/about
- Great Vibes — https://fonts.google.com/specimen/Great+Vibes/about
- Manrope — https://fonts.google.com/specimen/Manrope/about

These font families are offered under the SIL Open Font License 1.1. Bundled font files retain their internal copyright metadata. Official license text: https://openfontlicense.org/open-font-license-official-text/

Full license texts are included in `public/assets/fonts/licenses/`.
